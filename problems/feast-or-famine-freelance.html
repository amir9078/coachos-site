Feast or famine: the freelance cycle

The same question has been posted, in nearly the same words, for seven years. That’s not a motivation problem. It’s a structural one.

The public record

2018 asked it. 2021 asked it again, word for word.

FR
“One moment you’re drowning in work. The next you’re begging for any project to come up but it doesn’t.”
A freelancer on r/freelanceWriters, 2018
FR
Three years later, the same question again — near-identical words, different person, same cycle.
r/freelance, 2021

When thousands of unrelated people describe their year in the same sentence for seven years, the cause isn’t any of them. The cycle is built into how solo work is structured: marketing only happens when delivery stops — which means the famine is already scheduled during the feast.
