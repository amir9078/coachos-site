Asked and answered

Three questions coaches ask next

What does done-for-you marketing for coaches include?

The calendar itself: three posts a week in your voice, plus content marketing for the longer pieces. A specialist drafts and reviews everything; you approve before anything ships under your name. The scope is written, with a fixed price.

How is this different from hiring a marketing agency?

It’s a different category. Not an agency, not another app — the middle ground: a specialist runs your marketing on our platform, you approve everything, it ships under your name. You never lose the pen; you just stop holding it alone.

Will it sound like AI wrote it?

No — and that’s a rule, not a hope. AI makes our specialists faster; it never makes the decisions. A named specialist reviews every draft, and you approve every piece before it publishes.
