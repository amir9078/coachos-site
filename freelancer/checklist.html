The Solo Business AI-Agent Starter Checklist.

Twelve concrete steps for setting up your first AI-assisted follow-up, invoice reminder, and content workflow — usable with or without CoachOS. Print it, or save this page.

“A problem well put is half solved.”

John Dewey

Leads & proposals

✓

1. Write down your actual follow-up rule for quiet proposals

Decide, in writing, how many days after a proposal goes silent you follow up, and with what tone — a client "checking the budget" needs a nudge, not a re-pitch.

✓

2. Keep one pipeline for every lead and proposal, not your inbox

Stage, value, next step — as long as "where is this deal and what happens next" is answerable in ten seconds.

✓

3. Note what actually converts, by source

Direct outreach vs. referral vs. platform lead — a short note on each closed deal shows you where to stop wasting time.

Project delivery

✓

4. Put scope and deliverables in writing before work starts, every time

This one habit prevents more lost margin than any pricing strategy — no exceptions, even for a "quick, small job."

✓

5. Send a short status update at every milestone, not just at delivery

Clients who hear nothing assume nothing is happening — a two-line update costs you a minute and buys you trust.

✓

6. Track revisions used against revisions included in scope

Flag it before you're three rounds deep for free — a visible count keeps "just one more small change" from eating your rate.

Billing

✓

7. Set a deposit, a milestone schedule, and a late-payment reminder cadence

Deposit up front, invoice on delivery or by milestone, then day 3/7/14 reminders — decide this once, stop renegotiating it project to project.

✓

8. Track retainer hours or credits used, not just the total

Know when a client's at 80% of their retainer before the hours run out — that's your renewal conversation, not an awkward overage invoice.

Marketing & content

✓

9. Keep a swipe file of client questions and objections

That's your next case study, FAQ, or landing page line — write it down as it comes up, don't invent topics from scratch.

✓

10. Set one realistic cadence for showing your work and hold it

A monthly portfolio update beats a weekly post that dies in six weeks — pick the cadence you'll actually keep.

The agentic layer

✓

11. Decide what you'll never let an AI send unreviewed

Draft anything you like with AI — but a client-facing message that goes out wrong can cost you the next contract, so decide up front what always gets a human read first.
