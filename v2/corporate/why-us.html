The honest claim

We haven't found another company doing exactly this combination

Mentoring software gives you a matching engine and a dashboard, then leaves the design and the reporting narrative to you. Consulting firms hand you a launch plan and invoice for the deck. We looked, and we haven't found one vendor that does all three — designs the program, runs the day-to-day operations with AI, and supplies vetted external bench when internal capacity runs short — under one roof, accountable to the same team. If you've found one that genuinely does, we'd like to know; that's not a claim we take lightly.
