How every piece of work happens

Three hands on everything. Yours is the last.

A personal AI that knows your program’s criteria and goals sits inside every workflow. The loop holds at any scale:

01

The agent does the work. It reads the situation first — the pair’s history, the session cadence, the survey signal — then does the job: drafts the check-in, flags the quiet pair, assembles the quarterly report. Finished work, not suggestions.

02

Our specialist shapes it with the agent. A person from our team works alongside the AI on what matters — tone, judgment, the read a machine can’t make. Two sets of hands before it ever reaches you.

03

You approve in one click. Everything lands in one queue, under your name. Yes sends it. Edit changes it. Nothing — an email, a post, a contract, a charge — ever moves without you.

Inside the Desk

One screen for the whole program

Straight from the product, not a promise.

Program health, session counts, and pairs at risk on one view — with the follow-ups and check-ins already drafted underneath. Your program admin approves in one click; nothing reaches an employee unreviewed.

End to end, automatically

What runs itself across every pair

01

Matching. Pairs proposed on your criteria — skills, goals, conflicts checked — approved by your team, never auto-assigned.

02

Tracking. Sessions log themselves; nobody chases mentors for attendance spreadsheets again.

03

Early flags. A pair gone quiet for three weeks is flagged now — not discovered in the year-end survey.

04

Onboarding. Every new mentor gets the sequence — expectations, materials, first-session guide — without an admin sending it.

05

Check-ins. Nudges and pulse checks drafted and scheduled; your admin approves the wording once.

06

Reporting. The quarterly report assembles itself from real activity — participation, progress, outcomes — ready for one review.
