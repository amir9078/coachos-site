How it works

A directory that means something

A client searches by what actually matters — specialty, price range, language, video or in person — and chooses among people who all came through the same vetted door. The narrowing has already been done, so being found here means something.

Your listing is built from the positioning work we do together in the services, and it stays linked to your website and your Roster profile — one identity everywhere. And when someone enquires, it doesn’t go to a separate inbox you’ll forget to check: it lands straight on your own Desk leads board, where the follow-up is already drafted.

One number that matters: the Shortlist takes 0% of your revenue. Open directories charge 15–30% of every sale, and marketplace minimum fees can eat up to 28% of a smaller plan. This is a listing, never a commission. Your rate is your rate.
