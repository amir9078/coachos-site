How it works

A directory that means something

When a client searches the Shortlist, they’re choosing among people who all came through the same vetted door. That’s the whole product: the narrowing has already been done, so being found here means something.

Your listing is built from the positioning work we do together in the services — set it up once, and it goes live with your invite. It stays linked to your website and your Roster profile, so everything points to the same you.

And one number that matters: the Shortlist takes 0% of your revenue. Open directories charge 15–30% of every sale, and marketplace minimum fees can eat up to 28% of a smaller plan. Here, your rate is your rate.
