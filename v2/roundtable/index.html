Why this one is small on purpose

Free communities fill with beginners asking the same three questions. Paid ones fill with people who bought access and never showed up.

The Roundtable is small on purpose. Discussions and referrals with people running real practices — the referral network this line of work already runs on, gathered in one room instead of scattered group chats and old contacts. And when you’re ready to turn what you know into a course, our team builds it with you: curriculum, hosting, launch. Once you’re in, you’re in. No re-applying, no tiers.
