A feed with a spine

The rules that keep it worth reading

No AI posts on anyone’s behalf. Ever. Every word in this room was written or approved by the person whose name is on it — the same rule that governs everything on the Desk. A share always carries credit to the original author.

And moderation isn’t an afterthought bolted on once something goes wrong. Report and block exist from day one, filters catch spam before you see it, and a person reads what gets flagged. A feed is only as good as what it refuses to carry.
