04 / How it runs

AI does the drafting. A person signs off. Always.

Not a chatbot bolted onto old software, and never an AI publishing unsupervised.

1

The system drafts

Replies, posts, contracts, invoices, follow-ups — generated from your voice and your templates, the moment they’re needed.

2

A specialist reviews

A person on our team checks every draft before it goes anywhere. Anything off gets fixed or killed.

3

It ships under your name

You approve, it goes out as you. Your clients never talk to a bot — they get you, on time, every time.
