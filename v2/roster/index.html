Why a narrower network works

A connection request from a stranger with something to sell. An inbox that’s all noise. A profile format built for job-seekers when you’re not seeking a job — you’re running a practice.

The Roster is one profile built from your actual work — your specialty, your offers, your results. It links to your website and your Shortlist listing, so you maintain one identity instead of five. Connections and referrals mean something here for a simple reason: everyone was let in the same way you were.
