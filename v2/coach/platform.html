Your whole practice, run end to end. You just say yes.

This is not another CRM with your clients typed into it, and not one more tool on the pile. It’s one agentic system that runs the business around your coaching — from the first enquiry to the booked discovery call to the signed agreement to the session summary — while you’re in session.

How every piece of work happens

Three hands on everything. Yours is the last.

A personal AI that knows your practice and your goals sits inside every workflow. Here’s the loop that runs all day, on everything:

01

The agent does the work. It reads the situation first — the lead’s history, your rough session notes, the unpaid invoice — then does the job: drafts the follow-up, writes the client summary, queues the reminder. Finished work, not suggestions.

02

Our specialist shapes it with the agent. A person from our team works alongside the AI on what matters — tone, judgment, the read a machine can’t make. Two sets of hands before it ever reaches you.

03

You approve in one click. Everything lands in one queue, under your name. Yes sends it. Edit changes it. Nothing — an email, a post, a contract, a charge — ever moves without you.

Watch a session close itself

Straight from the product, not a promise.

Half-finished notes typed between sessions become a warm, clean summary for your client and a private action list for you — goals tracked, homework checked off. You read it, you press approve, it sends. That’s the whole job.

End to end, automatically

What runs itself while you’re in session

01

Enquiry. A new enquiry is answered in minutes and a discovery call lands on your calendar — reminders included.

02

Quiet lead. Gone silent after the call? Flagged on day six, with a warm follow-up already drafted in your voice.

03

Summary. Your messy post-session notes become the client email and your private note — drafted, waiting for your yes.

04

Homework. Mentioned homework? It’s on the client’s portal with gentle reminders before the next session.

05

Invoice. Session 12 of 12 coming up? The renewal nudge and the invoice are queued on your cadence.

06

Prep. Thirty minutes before each session: what they committed to, what stalled, what to open with. Your eyes only.
