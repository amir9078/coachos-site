Only in the coach fit

Built around the session

The unit of your business is the session, so the Desk is shaped around it. Enquiries get answered while you’re in one. A prep brief arrives thirty minutes before each one — what they committed to, what stalled, what to open with. The summary is drafted minutes after it ends. Even your calendar holds buffers between them, because back-to-back sessions are how good coaches burn out.
