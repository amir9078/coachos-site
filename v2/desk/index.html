The rule everything runs on

Nothing leaves this desk without you

The Desk never sends anything — an email, a post, a contract, a charge — without you seeing the exact words first and choosing to send them. AI drafts. You decide. Every time, no exceptions.

That’s not a limitation waiting to be removed in a later version. It’s the whole reason you can trust a system with your client relationships. Your name is on everything that goes out, so your hand is on everything that goes out.

The part that thinks

It reads the situation before it writes a word

Most AI features are one prompt in, one draft out. The Desk’s agent works differently. Point it at a lead who’s gone quiet and it reads the full history, checks how similar situations actually played out, decides what kind of attention this one needs — and only then drafts the move, in your voice. Then it stops.

You see its reasoning, not just its answer. Approve, edit, or dismiss. And the boundary holds at any scale: the agent can see across your leads, clients, bookings, and billing to catch what a single screen can’t — a package running low with no renewal sent, an overdue invoice ahead of tomorrow’s session — but wider sight never means wider authority. It can suggest more. It still can’t send, post, or charge a thing without you.

So membership works differently here: every member is onboarded by hand — your clients, templates, and calendar moved in with you, not dumped on you. The invite is the filter. It keeps every room worth being in, and it’s why the people inside are the reason to join.
