Why you can’t just sign up

Two reasons, both learned the hard way. Communities that are open to anyone who pays fill up with people who haven’t started yet, and quietly die. And most “AI platforms” are a chat box stapled onto old software — they save you an hour and then cost you two in new admin.

So we built this with a small founding group, onboarding each member by hand — and that’s still how it works. The invite is the filter. It keeps every room worth being in, and it’s why the people inside are the reason to join. We can’t invite everyone. That’s not a launch tactic; it’s the product.
