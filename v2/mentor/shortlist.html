How it works

A directory that means something

A client searches by what actually matters — specialty, price range, language, video or in person — and chooses among people who all came through the same vetted door. The narrowing has already been done, so being found here means something.

Your listing is built from the positioning work we do together in the services, and it stays linked to your website and your Roster profile — one identity everywhere. When someone enquires, it lands straight on your own Desk leads board, where the follow-up is already drafted.

One number that matters: the Shortlist takes 0% of your revenue. Open directories charge 15–30% of every sale, and marketplace minimum fees can eat up to 28% of a smaller plan. This is a listing, never a commission. And the proof on a listing is drawn from real client engagements, published only with that client’s explicit yes — no purchased reviews, no imported star ratings.

The Shortlist grows deliberately. A directory full of noise is worse than no directory — so it opens wider only as real, vetted supply comes in, never to hit a number.
