Roster.
The part nobody else built

Ask without your name on it

Every profession has questions its people can’t ask in public. What you really charge. The client relationship that’s gone wrong. The month you couldn’t pay yourself. On every other network those questions stay unasked, because your name — and your clients — are watching.

On the Roster, any member can post a question anonymously. The room sees the question, never the asker. The answers come back privately — only the asker reads them, nothing ever appears on their profile, and even we present it unlinked. It’s the confessional the profession never had, sitting inside the network it always needed.

And when you have something bigger to say than a post: write an article. Something smaller: a two-line take. The feed carries all of it — full posts, quick takes, articles, polls, and the questions nobody signs.
