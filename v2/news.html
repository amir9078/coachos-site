The coaching platform market just shrank by one

Practice.do — one of the better-funded 'all-in-one coach CRM' tools — shut down in November 2025, locking coaches out of their own client records with days of notice. It's the clearest sign yet that selling software alone isn't enough; the tool only matters if someone's still there to run it. This is why CoachOS pairs platform with a team.

Coaches are spending on tech, but not on the right layer

ICF's 2025 Global Coaching Study found just 19% of coach practitioners invested in new technology last year, while 54% say better tools are a priority. The gap isn't that coaches don't want tooling — it's that most of what shipped was one more dashboard to log into, not a system that runs anything for them.
