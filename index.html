04 / How it works

AI does the drafting. A person signs off. Always.

AI makes our specialists faster. It never makes the decisions. Nothing goes out without a person checking it. When readers even suspect a machine wrote something, they rate it 48% less trustworthy. That’s exactly why the review step exists. (This is the part we argue about internally the most.)

1

The system drafts

Replies, posts, contracts, invoices, follow-ups. Drafted from your voice and your templates the moment you need them.

2

A specialist reviews

A person on our team checks every draft before it goes anywhere. You will know their name. Anything that reads wrong gets fixed or dropped.

3

It ships under your name

You approve it, and it goes out as you. Your clients never talk to a bot. They get you, on time, every time.
