How many participants can a manually run mentoring program handle?

About 50. Manual matching takes roughly ten minutes a pair, and past 50 participants the matching, scheduling, and session tracking outgrow a spreadsheet. That is the point where programs either adopt a system or quietly fade.

Do mentoring programs actually improve retention?

Measurably. Mentees stay at 72% and mentors at 69%, against 49% for non-participants. Documented program ROI runs up to 600%, which is part of why 78%+ of the Fortune 500 run mentoring programs.
