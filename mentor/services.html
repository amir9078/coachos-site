How this actually runs

Every step is either drafted by AI or decided by a person — never both pretending to be one.

You

Tell us who you mentor and what you've actually done

Not a form. A real conversation about your background, who you're best positioned to help, and what you've seen work.

AI drafts

A first pass at your offer, your site copy, your positioning

Built from the actual conversation you just had, in your language — this is where most tools hand you a template instead.

You decide

What's right, what's wrong, what stays

You read every draft before it becomes real. Nothing about your offer ships without your sign-off.

AI executes

The site goes live, the booking flow works, follow-up starts running

The repetitive part — building, publishing, chasing replies — happens without you doing it by hand.

You stay in the loop

Every message to a mentee still gets your voice and your call

AI drafts the follow-up. It doesn't decide the tone, and it doesn't send anything you haven't approved once your process is set.
