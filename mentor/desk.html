One desk. Every room of the business.

Five tools. Five logins. Client info scattered everywhere — Desk is one record from enquiry to invoice. AI drafts, a specialist reviews the parts that matter, and it ships under your name.

Inside the Desk

Look at the screen, not at a promise

The same morning, four rooms. Notice what’s already done in each one.

The morning view — work finished overnight

You open one screen. The follow-up for the quiet lead is written. The session summary is drafted from your rough notes. The week’s post is scheduled. Your numbers and your daily briefing are waiting. Everything needs exactly one thing from you: a yes.

The pipeline — it notices before you do

Every enquiry sorts itself onto one board. When a lead goes quiet, the agent flags it, reads the full history, checks what worked in similar situations, and proposes the next move with its reasoning shown — then stops and waits for you.

Client delivery — messy notes in, client-ready out

Half-finished notes typed between sessions become a warm, clean summary for the client and a private action list for you — goals tracked, homework checked off, the whole engagement in one arc. You read it, you press approve, it sends.

Bookings — a calendar that defends itself

One link fills the week. Reminders go out on your rules, confirmations chase themselves, and a no-show gets a rebooking invite without you lifting a hand. Sessions run with recording and transcript built in.

Practice intelligence
