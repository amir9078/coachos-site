The problem

Ad hoc mentorship doesn't scale

A call here, a favor there — informal mentorship doesn't scale, doesn't get paid properly, and burns out the mentor first, usually before it ever becomes a real part of the business. Part of why: coaching has more or less an accepted package template. Mentorship doesn't — most mentors are guessing at format, cadence, and price with nothing to check their work against.

The solution

A structure that runs without you chasing it

We set up the structure — format, pricing, and a booking flow — so mentorship runs on its own once it's live, instead of depending on you to coordinate every session. Already running a mentorship somewhere else, informally or not? Bringing that structure over is part of the scope call, not a separate project.
