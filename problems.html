Website & Presence

If people can't find you, nothing else on this page matters

What we hear

“Is a Linktree enough, or do I actually need a real site?”

28% of small businesses still don't have a website going into 2026. Plenty of the ones that do haven't touched it since launch — no update, no clear answer to who it's actually for, nothing a search engine can find.

How we help

A site built around what you actually do, live and kept current

Website, hosting, domain, and a real business email — built around your specific offer, not a template, and maintained instead of frozen the day it launched.

Human + AI

Website, built for you

The problem: A site that's generic enough to fit anyone ends up meaning nothing to the one person actually looking for you.

How we solve it: AI drafts the structure and copy from the real conversation about what you do and who you help; you review every line before it goes live.

What it costs left unmanaged: A referral who searches your name and finds nothing — or a dead template — doesn't call to ask questions. They just move to the next name.

AI

Hosting & domain setup

The problem: DNS, SSL, and renewal dates are exactly the kind of task that gets put off until something breaks in public.

How we solve it: AI configures and monitors it — registration, security, renewal — so it's not a recurring task on your list at all.

How we solve it: AI sets up your real business address and the records behind it (so mail actually lands in inboxes, not spam) — done once, correctly.

What it costs left unmanaged: Every invoice and reply sent from a free address quietly costs credibility you never see being lost.

How we solve it: AI checks and claims consistent handles across platforms before you post once.

What it costs left unmanaged: A mismatched or unclaimed handle on even one platform makes people pause to check they've found the real you.

Leads & Follow-Up

Most leads don't go cold — they just never get a reply

What we hear

“I know there are inquiries sitting somewhere. I just don't always see them in time.”

63.5% of companies never respond to a lead at all. Among those that do, 74% miss the widely-used 5-minute response benchmark — response speed is one of the most consistently cited factors in whether a lead converts at all. The business that replies first tends to win the deal, buyer surveys put it around 78% of the time, regardless of who had the better pitch.

How we help

One inbox for every lead, followed up before it goes cold

Every enquiry lands in one place, scored and followed up on a timer — not whenever you next remember to check five different inboxes.

Human + AI

Lead generation

The problem: Without a real system, "marketing" and "lead generation" are the same neglected task — the same root cause as the 47%/81% self-management numbers above.

How we solve it: AI runs the actual generation channels — social, content, referral prompts — on a schedule instead of whenever there's time.

What it costs left unmanaged: No real pipeline means no real choice — you take whichever client shows up, not the ones you actually want.

AI

Lead management

The problem: 63.5% of companies never respond to a lead at all; among those that do, most miss the window where a reply still converts.

How we solve it: Every enquiry lands on one board, scored automatically, with follow-up sequences that run on the timing you set.

What it costs left unmanaged: The deal isn't lost to a competitor with a better pitch — it's lost to whoever simply replied first.

How we help

The follow-up that actually happens, on schedule, in your voice

Structured, persistent follow-up and closing support — so “let me think about it” gets a real second and third touch instead of quietly becoming a no.

Human + AI

Sales & closing support

The problem: Most reps quit follow-up long before a prospect is actually ready to say no — the gap between "interested" and "paying" is where deals quietly die.

How we solve it: AI drafts the follow-up sequence and tells you exactly when to send it; you have the actual conversation and make the close.

What it costs left unmanaged: Every unfollowed-up "maybe" is a sale you already did the hard part of — the pitch — and then gave away for free.

Good intentions don't scale without a system underneath them

What we hear

“We paired the right people. Somehow that still wasn't enough.”

Structured mentoring succeeds 91% of the time, versus 30% left to run informally. At company scale that gap becomes 80+ hours of manual admin work per cycle, and only 11% of HR & L&D teams feel confident in their current strategy.

How we help

Structure, matching, and reporting — built in, not bolted on

For individual mentors: positioning, program setup, and mentee matching. For companies: program design, automated matching, tracking, and the reporting leadership actually reads.

For individual mentors

Human + AI

Mentor positioning & offer setup

The problem: 76% of people say having a mentor matters; only 37% actually have one — a gap that's partly just unclear, unfindable offers.

How we solve it: AI drafts your positioning and offer page from a real conversation about your background; you approve it.

What it costs left unmanaged: A mentor with real experience and no clear offer gets skipped for someone less qualified but easier to understand.

Human + AI

The problem: Structured programs succeed 91% of the time; left informal, that drops to 30%.

How we solve it: AI handles the format, pricing, and booking logistics so the program runs on its own once it's set.

What it costs left unmanaged: An unstructured "let's grab coffee sometime" mentorship rarely survives past the first meeting.

How we solve it: AI runs matching against real criteria and lists you in a directory mentees actually search.
