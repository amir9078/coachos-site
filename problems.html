Website & Presence

If people can't find you, nothing else here matters

What people tell us

I kept telling myself the website was “good enough” — it went up two years ago and I never touched it again. A friend told me she'd looked me up before referring a client, saw the old headshot and a paragraph that didn't even mention what I specialize in now, and just didn't send the referral. She mentioned it almost as an aside. I have no idea how many other times that happened without anyone telling me. I'd have fixed it the first month it went stale, not two years in.

How we fix it

The site is rebuilt around what you actually do right now, and updating it is part of the service, not a task you have to remember. Our AI system drafts every update from the same conversation we're already having about your work; a specialist on our team checks it reads like you and matches your actual offer before anything goes live.

What people tell us

My domain lapsed for four days because the renewal email went to a folder I never check. In that window someone registered it and tried to sell it back to me for eight times what I originally paid. I got it back, but for four days my business email bounced and my site just didn't exist. I would have paid for a renewal reminder that actually worked.

How we fix it

Domain, hosting, and SSL are monitored and renewed automatically. Our AI system tracks expiry dates, certificate status, and uptime around the clock and flags anything before it becomes visible; a specialist actually handles the renewal — it isn't left to an autoresponder hoping you notice in time.

What people tell us

How we fix it

Marketing & Visibility

The marketing stops the moment you get busy — that's the actual pattern

What people tell us

I post like clockwork for two, three weeks — then a full client roster happens, and the feed just goes dark. Not on purpose. I look up six weeks later and the last post is a promo for something that already ended. Someone who found me during that quiet stretch probably assumed I'd closed. I'll never know how many did — I'm one of the 47% of small business owners doing this entirely by hand.

How we fix it

The calendar runs whether or not you have a free hour this week. Our AI system drafts posts in your voice, on schedule, from what you're actually doing; a specialist reviews the batch before anything publishes, so it still sounds like you and never goes dark during your busiest month.

What people tell us

I have a list of a few hundred people who opted in because they liked something I said once. I haven't emailed them in five months. If I sent something today, half of them probably wouldn't remember signing up. That list was worth something. Now it's mostly just sitting there.

How we fix it

Our AI system drafts the newsletter on a real schedule from what's already happening in your work, so you're never staring at a blank draft. A specialist checks tone and facts before it sends, so the list stays warm instead of quietly going cold.

What people tell us

Someone asked me at an event who I actually work with, and I gave a two-minute answer that trailed off halfway through. I watched their eyes glaze over. They didn't ask a follow-up question. I've thought about that exact moment more times than I'd like to admit — I know exactly what I should have said, weeks too late.

How we fix it

Our AI system drafts a handful of specific, sayable positioning options from a real conversation about your work and results; you pick the one that's actually you. A specialist stress-tests it against “would a stranger repeat this correctly” before it goes anywhere.

What people tell us

How we fix it

Our AI system drafts long-form content from your actual expertise and notes on a schedule that doesn't depend on motivation; a specialist edits every piece before it ships, so the volume that actually moves rankings happens regardless of your energy that week.

This is usually the first thing that slips. None of these have to run on your energy levels.

Leads & Follow-Up

Most leads don't go cold — they just never get a reply

What people tell us

I genuinely don't know where my last three clients came from. Referral, maybe, or someone who saw an old post. I have no system, so I have no idea what's actually working, which means I can't do more of it on purpose. I just wait and hope the phone rings again.

How we fix it

Our AI system runs your actual generation channels — social, content, referral prompts — on a real schedule, and tracks what's bringing people in. A specialist reviews what's working monthly and adjusts, so growing on purpose replaces waiting and hoping.

What people tell us

I found a message from a potential client sitting in my Instagram DMs for eleven days. Eleven. I don't check that inbox regularly. By the time I replied, apologizing, she'd already booked with someone else — who probably answered in under an hour. I lost a client to my own inbox, not to competition. 63.5% of companies never respond to a lead at all; I was almost one of them.

How we fix it

Every enquiry — DM, form, email — lands in one place the moment it arrives, scored, with follow-up that runs on a timer. Our AI system catches what a five-inbox habit misses; a specialist handles anything that actually needs judgment, so nothing sits for eleven days again.

63.5% never-respond figure: RevenueHero, 1,000 B2B SaaS companies (2024). First-responder win rate and 5-minute benchmark: Blazeo 2026 speed-to-lead study, 573 businesses.

Response speed is the single most fixable thing here. This is usually live within days, not weeks.

Sales & Closing

The deal didn't fall through. Nobody followed up on it

What people tell us

She said “let me think about it and get back to you” six weeks ago. I never followed up, because it felt pushy, and now I'm not sure if reaching out again looks desperate or professional. I did the hard part — the actual pitch — and then just let it evaporate because I didn't know what to say next. Turns out that's most people: 48% of salespeople never make a single follow-up attempt, and 80% of deals that do close needed five or more.

How we fix it

We build the follow-up sequence and tell you exactly when to send it and what to say — the actual conversation is still yours, but the “did I wait too long, is this weird now” guesswork is gone. Our AI system drafts the touchpoints on schedule; a specialist makes sure the tone never reads as pushy before you send anything.

The pitch was never the problem. The follow-up almost always is.

Programs & Structure

Good intentions don't scale without a system underneath them

For individual mentors

What people tell us

I've mentored people informally for years, but I don't have anything you could call an offer — no page, no price, no name for it. Someone asked me last month if I take on mentees and I genuinely didn't know how to answer. 76% of people say having a mentor matters; only 37% actually have one, and I'm part of why that gap exists on the mentor side too.

How we fix it

Our AI system drafts a real offer and page from a conversation about your actual background and results; a specialist checks it's specific enough that someone could describe it to a friend in one sentence — which is the whole test.

What people tell us

It was always just “grab coffee sometime.” No format, no cadence, no clear end point. Most of those relationships fizzled after one or two meetings — not because the fit was wrong, but because nothing was actually scheduled to happen next. Structured programs succeed 91% of the time; left informal, that's 30%.

How we fix it

Our AI system handles the format, pricing, and booking logistics so the program runs on rails instead of good intentions; a specialist sets it up around how you actually want to mentor, not a generic template.

What people tell us

People have told me for years I should mentor formally — I have the experience for it. I just have no idea how someone looking for a mentor in my specific field would ever find me. I'm not on any list anywhere.

How we fix it

Our AI system matches you against real criteria — specialty, goals, availability — inside a directory people looking for a mentor actually search. A specialist reviews the matches before an intro is made, so it's not a cold algorithm decision.

Our AI system runs the matching, onboarding, and nudges automatically; a specialist checks the criteria and the exceptions, but the 9pm spreadsheet sessions are gone entirely.

Our AI system compiles participation and outcome data into a report that's ready before the review, not scrambled together after. A specialist checks it before it reaches leadership, so it's a real number, not a guess dressed up.
