And the two problems no tool on that list solves:

01

They don’t talk to each other. The same client scattered across separate databases, with you re-typing the name between them. Every “integration” you wire up is one more thing that silently breaks.

02

None of them acts. Even perfectly connected, every tool on that list is passive — it holds information and waits. Nothing notices the quiet lead, drafts the follow-up, or preps the session. That’s the agentic gap: software you operate versus a system that operates for you.

The Desk closes both at once: one record for everything, and an agent with hands — checked by our specialist, approved by you. Starting from zero? Even the website, the domain, and the business email begin here.

How every piece of work happens

Three hands on everything. Yours is the last.

01

The agent does the work. Reads the situation — the lead’s history, your rough session notes, the unpaid invoice — then finishes the job: the follow-up, the client summary, the reminder. Done, not suggested.

02

Our specialist shapes it. A person from our team, on everything that matters — tone, judgment, the read a machine can’t make.

03

You click yes. One queue, one click. Nothing — an email, a post, a contract, a charge — moves without it.

Only in the coach fit

Built around the session

The unit of your business is the session, so the Desk is shaped around it. Enquiries get answered while you’re in one. A prep brief arrives thirty minutes before each one — what they committed to, what stalled, what to open with. The summary is drafted minutes after it ends. Even your calendar holds buffers between them, because back-to-back sessions are how good coaches burn out.
