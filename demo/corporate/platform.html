And the two problems no tool on that list solves:

01

They don’t talk to each other. The same pair scattered across separate databases, with you re-typing the name between them. Every “integration” you wire up is one more thing that silently breaks.

02

None of them acts. Even perfectly connected, every tool on that list is passive — it holds information and waits. Nothing notices the quiet pair, drafts the check-in, or assembles the report. That’s the agentic gap: software you operate versus a system that operates for you.

The Desk closes both at once: one record per pair, and an agent that does the chasing — shaped by our specialists, with your admin approving every send. The firewall on personal content is contractual.

How every piece of work happens

Three hands on everything. Yours is the last.

01

The agent does the work. Reads the situation — the pair’s history, the session cadence, the survey signal — then finishes the job: the check-in, the flag, the assembled report. Done, not suggested.

02

Our specialist shapes it. A person from our team, on everything that matters — tone, judgment, the read a machine can’t make.

03

You click yes. One queue, one click. Nothing — an email, a post, a contract, a charge — moves without it.
