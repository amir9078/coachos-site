And the two problems no tool on that list solves:

01

They don’t talk to each other. The lead lives in your inbox, the call in Calendly, the contract in the e-sign tool, the invoice in another tab — the same client scattered across seven databases, with you re-typing her name between them. Every “integration” you wire up is one more thing that silently breaks.

02

None of them acts. Even perfectly connected, every tool on that list is passive — it holds information and waits for you. Nothing reads the situation, notices the quiet lead, drafts the chase, or preps the session. That’s the agentic gap: software you operate versus a system that operates for you.

Desk closes both gaps at once: one record from enquiry to invoice, and AI that drafts the admin — never replaces the human in the room. Starting from zero? Launch Services cover website, domain, and business email before Desk opens.

The integration, shown

One client, end to end. Her name typed once.

02

03

04

05

06

Between sessions she keeps hearing from you — the newsletter in your voice, drafted from real, anonymized themes, approved in batches.

07

Session 10 of 12 — the renewal conversation is queued before the goodbye session, and the invoice follows the yes.

08

Every step landed in one record — her history, agreements, invoices, and progress in one place. You typed her name exactly once, at step one.

That’s the difference between nine tools synced and one system: the tools would each need telling. Here, each step already knows.
