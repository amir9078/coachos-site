01

02

None of them acts. Even perfectly connected, every tool on that list is passive — it holds information and waits for you. Nothing reads the situation, notices the quiet lead, drafts the chase, or preps the session. That’s the agentic gap: software you operate versus a system that operates for you.

The Desk closes both at once: one record for everything, and an agent with hands — checked by our specialist, approved by you. Starting from zero? Even the website, the domain, and the business email begin here.

The integration, shown

One client, end to end. Her name typed once.

01

She finds you — through your site or your Shortlist listing. Her enquiry lands on your leads board, not in a portal you forget to check.

02

03

04

05

06

07

08

Every step landed in one record — her history, agreements, invoices, and progress in one place. You typed her name exactly once, at step one.

That’s the difference between nine tools synced and one system: the tools would each need telling. Here, each step already knows.

01 — Leads

An enquiry from your website, social media, email, or your Shortlist listing lands as an Inquiry — AI-scored, promoted to a Lead, and placed on the board: New → Talked → Proposal → Won. The first conversation, every follow-up, and every reminder runs from that one record. Goes quiet six days? The agent reads the history and proposes the next move — drafted, reasoned, waiting for your yes.
