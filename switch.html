Switching

Your platform shut down. Your business didn’t.

Practice.do is gone. Profi is gone. Agencies raise prices mid-contract, and software you loved gets acquired and gutted. Moving to CoachOS is part of onboarding — not a second project you have to find time for.

Why this page exists

Buyers in this market now ask a harder question

Not “what does it do?” — “will you exist next year?” After two platform shutdowns in this space, that’s the right question, and it deserves a straight answer instead of a logo wall.

Ours: CoachOS is independent and revenue-funded — no venture clock forcing a sale or a shutdown. Pricing is flat and written into your scope. Your data is exportable from day one: your site, your client list, your pipeline, your contracts, your content. If we ever part ways, you leave with everything. We’d rather earn the renewal than lock the door.

Three ways people arrive

Where are you switching from?

1

A platform that died — or looks shaky

We rebuild your client records, scheduling, and contracts on CoachOS, and put your export policy in writing so this never happens to you twice. Bring whatever exports you have; we’ll sort the mess.

2

An agency retainer that stopped making sense

A boutique agency runs $24,000 a year; done-for-you tiers start at $500 a month and are built for businesses doing $30K+ monthly. The middle ground — a specialist plus software, everything approved by you — is the category we run. Same work, without the overhead you were really paying for.

3

Doing everything yourself

“DIY works for a week — I don’t stick with it.” A business owner wrote that, and 54% of owners still run all their own marketing. Switching from DIY means handing over the calendar, not control: you approve everything before it ships, under your name.

What the move includes

Migration is onboarding, not a project

Client list, contracts, calendar, content, and domains come over as part of setup. Nothing goes dark mid-move: your site stays up, your booked sessions stay booked, and every inquiry that lands during the switch still gets answered in minutes — that habit starts on day one, not after launch.

You get a written scope with a fixed price before anything starts, and a named specialist who answers for the move end to end.
