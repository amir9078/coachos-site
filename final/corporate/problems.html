Problems we fix

Research-backed problems. Clear fixes.

CoachOS builds and runs website, marketing, and lead follow-up for coaches, mentors, freelancers, and small businesses. Below: the problems we hear most, with sources you can check and the service that fixes each one.

25–50

Manual matching tops out at 25–50 people — most cohorts are bigger

HR
“Cohort one was 32 people and I matched them in an evening. Cohort two was 58 and took a whole weekend — and on Sunday night I found I’d matched someone with her own line manager. Cohort three is meant to be 90.”
HR lead · composite account

The answerThe whole cohort matched in days on your criteria, with conflicts caught before pairing. → Matching

98%

Sign-ups get counted; whether it helps anyone is a separate, unasked question

LD
“We launched in March and 240 people signed up, which everyone treated as the result. In October I went through the logs — about half the pairs had met once. Once, and then nothing. I still put the sign-up number in the quarterly deck.”
L&D manager · composite account

The answerSession tracking with early flags — you know in week six which pairs went quiet, not at the survey. → Tracking

4.2/5

A satisfaction score is not a number a CFO can act on

LD
“The finance review is in November and the only real number I have is 4.2 out of 5, from a survey most people ignored. The CFO asked what would happen if we didn’t run it, and I could hear how thin my answer sounded.”
L&D manager · composite account

The answerParticipation, goals met, and outcomes per pair — a report you can put next to the budget line it defends. → Reporting

83%

Mentee demand is rising; the pool of willing mentors is not keeping pace

HR
“We opened mentee sign-ups and got 140 in a week. Mentor sign-ups: 31, eleven of them the same people as last year. So there’s a waitlist I don’t advertise.”
HR lead · composite account

The answerMentor onboarding that keeps volunteers, and vetted external mentors from the Shortlist when the pool runs short. → The Platform

How this was put together: every account is a realistic composite written to show the pattern clearly, not a verbatim quote from one person. The statistics are checked against their original sources — anything we couldn’t verify was cut. The full research, across every fit, is on the main problems page.

Every answer above is a service we run today.
