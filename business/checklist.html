The Solo Business AI-Agent Starter Checklist.

Twelve concrete steps for setting up your first AI-assisted follow-up, invoice reminder, and content workflow — usable with or without CoachOS. Print it, or save this page.

“A problem well put is half solved.”

John Dewey

Leads & follow-up

✓

1. Write down your actual follow-up rule for quotes gone quiet

Decide, in writing, how many days after a quote you follow up, and with what tone — most quotes are lost to silence, not price.

✓

2. Keep one place for every enquiry and quote, not your inbox or a stack of texts

A spreadsheet, a simple board, anything — as long as "where did this lead come from and what happened" is answerable in ten seconds.

✓

3. Note which lead source actually turns into booked work

Reviews, referrals, ads — a short note on each booked job shows you where your next dollar of marketing should go.

Job & appointment delivery

✓

4. Confirm every appointment or job the day before

Automated or not, a same-day-before confirmation is the single biggest fix for the no-shows that quietly eat a service business's revenue.

✓

5. Send a short summary or receipt right after the job, same day

It's also the best moment to ask for a review, while the work is still fresh in the customer's mind.

✓

6. Track repeat-customer history somewhere visible to the whole team

Who's due for another service, who hasn't been back in six months — "it's in my head" doesn't scale past one person.

Billing

✓

7. Set a fixed invoice-reminder schedule for anything not paid on the spot

Day 3, day 7, day 14 — pick numbers and stick to them, so chasing payment stops being an awkward judgment call every time.

✓

8. Track prepaid packages, memberships, or punch cards, not just totals

Flag it the moment someone hits 80% used before the card's already full — that's your renewal window, not after.

Marketing & content

✓

9. Keep a running list of the questions customers ask at the counter or on the phone

That's your best FAQ and content source, verbatim — write it down before you forget it, don't invent topics from scratch.

✓

10. Set one realistic posting/sending cadence and hold it

Monthly and consistent beats weekly and abandoned after six weeks — pick the cadence you'll actually keep.

The agentic layer

✓

11. Decide what you'll never let an AI send unreviewed

Draft anything you like with AI — but a wrong automated text to a customer costs a relationship, not just a message, so decide up front what always gets a human read first.
