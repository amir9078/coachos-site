Why Evensteer, specifically.

A plain answer for the team that runs the program: the category we operate, the rule we work by, and whether we’ll exist next year — so you can judge it yourself.

The short version

Not an agency. Not another app. The middle ground.

A specialist runs your mentoring program on our platform — matching, tracking, reporting — you approve everything, and it ships under your name. That’s the whole category, in one sentence.

The usual options don’t do this. Consultancies design a program and disappear. Mentoring software hands your admin one more login — and manual programs still strain past 50 participants, because the software doesn’t do the matching at ten minutes a pair; a person does. The gap between “a tool exists” and “someone runs it for me” is the seat we fill: the whole cycle, run and answered for by one team.

If you’ve found one company that genuinely does all of that, we’d like to know. That’s not a claim we make lightly.

The AI question, answered first

AI makes our specialists faster. It never makes the decisions.

A founder recently wrote about firing her agency: “They weren’t using AI as a tool. They were outsourcing their taste to it… I want AI to make talented people faster. I don’t want it to make mediocre work cheaper.”

Your employees are her, at scale. Content people merely suspect is machine-written rates 48% less trustworthy, 57% less authentic, and carries 60% less emotional connection — suspicion alone does the damage, and a mentoring program runs on trust or it doesn’t run. So nothing here ships on AI’s judgment: AI drafts. A specialist reviews. You approve. It ships as you. Every check-in, every match note, every report carries a named specialist’s sign-off before your people see it.
