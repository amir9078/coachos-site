The problems these fix

What enterprise programs lose at scale

50

Manual programs strain past 50 participants. “I can’t manage this if it grows beyond 50 people. I’m already stretched thin” — a real program manager, matching mentors in a spreadsheet. Matching alone runs about ten minutes a pair.

72%

Mentees stay at 72%. Non-participants: 49%. The retention case is already made — what most programs can’t show is that it happened in their building, because sign-ups get counted and outcomes don’t.

600%

Documented mentoring-program ROI runs up to 600%. Yet the quarterly deck usually shows a satisfaction score from a survey most people ignored. The gap is reporting, not results.
