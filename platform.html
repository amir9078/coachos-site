The rule all four run on

Nothing goes out without you

Across every room, one loop holds. The agent does the work — it reads the situation, then drafts the reply, the post, the reminder. A specialist from our team shapes it with the agent, adding the judgment a machine can’t. And the member approves in one click: yes sends it, edit changes it, and nothing — an email, a post, a charge — ever moves without them. Three hands on everything; the member’s is the last. Every time, no exceptions.
